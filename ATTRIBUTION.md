Based on [amusi/CVPR2026-Papers-with-Code](https://github.com/amusi/CVPR2026-Papers-with-Code), compiled by amusi, the CVer community, and the original repository's contributors.

Source: commit [`5709455e269a5d90809ecaa91082c02f2ea4b60f`](https://github.com/amusi/CVPR2026-Papers-with-Code/tree/5709455e269a5d90809ecaa91082c02f2ea4b60f), dated March 8, 2026.

This version translates the CVPR 2026 categories and navigation into English. It preserves all 31 paper listings, including one paper appearing in two categories, and their original paper, code, model, and project links. Broken navigation and title formatting were corrected. Promotional material and links to other conference collections were omitted.

“Link not listed” means the source did not supply a link; it does not establish whether one is available elsewhere. Entries and external link availability have not been independently reverified. Updates are maintained manually.

The upstream version does not include a license file. Papers and linked repositories remain subject to their authors' terms.
